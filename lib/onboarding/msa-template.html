ANNEXURE-H - POS TRANSACTION AND SETTLEMENT TERMS

This Annexure-H sets out the operational terms governing card and other payment Transactions processed by the Agent (referred to in its capacity as a merchant as the “Merchant”) through POS Devices allocated under this Agreement. It supplements Clauses 7 to 16 and Clause 20 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Annexure-H have the meanings given to them in Clause 1 of the Agreement.

H.1 Transaction Limits

H.1.1 The maximum permissible amount for any single Transaction processed through a POS Device is INR 1,19,999 (Rupees One Lakh Nineteen Thousand Nine Hundred Ninety-Nine Only).

H.1.2 Any Transaction exceeding the limit in paragraph H.1.1 shall be liable to be placed on Hold under Clause 13, and its proceeds may be withheld from Settlement for up to ninety (90) days pending verification by the Company and/or the acquiring bank.

H.1.3 The Company and the acquiring bank may prescribe lower per-transaction, daily, or monthly limits, MCC-based restrictions, or velocity limits from time to time under Clause 7.7, which shall bind the Merchant upon notification.

H.2 Genuine Transactions and Card Usage

H.2.1 Every Transaction shall represent a bona fide sale of goods or supply of services by the Merchant at its actual value, supported by an invoice or equivalent record. The Merchant shall not process any fictitious, circular, or accommodation Transaction, or any Transaction whose purpose is to provide cash to the cardholder, and any such Transaction shall constitute a breach of Clauses 7.5, 23.2, and 24.3.

H.2.2 Repeated Transactions using the same card on the same POS Device are strictly prohibited. A card may be used for only one Transaction at a time, and the Merchant shall not split a single sale into multiple Transactions. Repeated or split Transactions may be excluded from Settlement and placed on Hold.

H.3 Settlement Cycle and Cut-off

H.3.1 POS Settlement shall be made on a T+1 basis, and the Company shall endeavour to credit eligible proceeds between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM IST on the settlement day.

H.3.2 Notwithstanding the definition of “Business Day” in Clause 1.1.9, POS Settlement shall be processed on every calendar day, including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, save and except (a) Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), and Gandhi Jayanti (2 October); and (b) any day on which settlement is prevented or delayed by a bank, NPCI, or card network outage or other bank-related issue, in which case settlement shall be made on the next day on which it can be processed.

H.3.3 Transactions completed up to 10:00 PM IST shall be treated as Transactions of that day. Transactions completed after 10:00 PM IST shall be treated as Transactions of the next day, and their Settlement shall accordingly be deferred by one (1) day.

H.3.4 This paragraph H.3 is subject to Clauses 10, 11, 13, and 14 and to the Company’s right to withhold ineligible, disputed, or fraud-flagged amounts.

H.4 Cardholder Identification

H.4.1 Before processing a Transaction, the Merchant shall collect and verify the identity of the cardholder by obtaining the cardholder’s PAN and an officially valid identity document, and shall ensure that the identity details collected belong to the same person in whose name the card used for that Transaction is issued.

H.4.2 Where the cardholder voluntarily offers Aadhaar as the identity document, it shall be accepted only in masked form (with no more than the last four digits visible) or through offline verification. The Merchant shall not insist on Aadhaar as the sole acceptable document, shall not record or store the full Aadhaar number, and shall not capture any biometric information for this purpose.

H.4.3 Identity details shall be collected only after giving notice and obtaining consent in accordance with Clause 28 and Annexure-G, shall be used only for verification, dispute-resolution, and regulatory purposes, and shall be retained in accordance with Clauses 14.6 and 24.5.

H.5 Refunds and Chargebacks

H.5.1 Refunds shall be processed only in accordance with Applicable Law, card network rules, and the Company’s SOPs, only against a Transaction that was successfully completed and verified, and only to the original card used for that Transaction.

H.5.2 In the event of a Chargeback or disputed Transaction, the Merchant shall furnish all documentation required to contest or resolve the dispute, including the invoice, charge slip, cardholder identity details collected under paragraph H.4, and proof of delivery, within the timeline stipulated by the Company. Failing this, the Company may accept the Chargeback and recover the amount under Clauses 14 and 15.

H.6 Card Data Security and Confidentiality

H.6.1 The Merchant shall comply with all security standards prescribed by the Company, the acquiring bank, and the card networks, including encryption and secure data handling, to protect cardholder payment information.

H.6.2 The Merchant shall not store card data, including the full card number, CVV/CVC, PIN, expiry date, or magnetic stripe or chip data, in any form, save as expressly permitted under RBI directions on card data storage and tokenisation, and shall not share any cardholder payment details with any unauthorized person.

H.7 POS Device Custody and Liability

H.7.1 The Merchant shall keep each POS Device in a secure location, restrict access to authorized personnel only, and take all reasonable precautions against damage, theft, and misuse, in accordance with Clause 8.

H.7.2 Where a POS Device is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond economic repair owing to the Merchant’s negligence or failure to follow security protocols, the Merchant shall pay the Device Recovery Fee of INR 16,000 plus applicable GST per Device under Clause 9.4. Where the damage is repairable, the Merchant shall bear the actual cost of repair, not exceeding the Device Recovery Fee. Such amounts may be recovered in the manner set out in Clauses 9.5 and 15.

H.8 POS Rental

H.8.1 Rental for each POS Device shall be as specified in Annexure-B and shall be payable monthly in advance.

H.8.2 Rental for the month in which a POS Device is activated shall be charged on a pro-rated basis from the date of activation to the last day of that month. Thereafter, rental shall be charged for each calendar month in advance, on or about the fifth (5th) day of that month.

H.8.3 The Merchant authorizes the Company to debit rental and applicable GST from its Wallet or Settlement under Clause 15.1(f). Rental shall be payable for each month or part thereof until the POS Device is returned in accordance with Clause 9.3.
